Recent Project · Cedar Roof

From Old Shingles to a Glowing Cedar Roof

For a few years we kept this family's tired shingle roof going with careful repairs, so they could plan for the replacement. When the time came, the crew redid the whole roof in cedar while they were away on spring break.

  • Repairs first, no pressure to replace
  • Chosen over several other estimates
  • New cedar roof, done while they were away

Recent Project · Chimney Repair

Stopping a Chimney Leak at the Source

Most chimney leaks start where the brick meets the roof. Here the old flashing came off and new metal flashing went in, set into the mortar joints and stepped down along the shingles.

  • Old, failing flashing removed
  • New flashing set into the brick joints
  • Stepped down along the shingles

Recent Project · Copper Gutters

Copper Gutters and Downspouts

Bright new copper along the eaves and down the corners of the house. Copper needs no paint and slowly weathers to a deep brown, then green, patina over the years.

  • New copper gutters at the eaves
  • Copper downspouts to the ground
  • No painting, ever
